🔒 Privacy Policy

Learn how SkillX collects, uses, protects, and manages your personal data.

Last Updated: September 2026

📌 1. Information Collected

We collect essential information required to connect students for peer-to-peer skill exchanges and maintain a safe community platform:

  • Account Details: Full name, email address, department, academic year, and profile picture or avatar emoji.
  • Skills & Interests: Skills you offer to teach and skills you wish to learn.
  • Platform Activity: Exchange requests, chat messages, shared notes, session schedules, and support/feedback inquiries.

🚀 2. How Data is Used

Your information is used solely to power SkillX features and improve your learning experience:

  • Matching you with relevant student teachers and peer learners.
  • Managing time credits, scheduling sessions, and facilitating real-time messaging.
  • Maintaining account security, preventing abuse, and providing customer support.
  • Enhancing platform performance and user interface experience.

🛡️ 3. Data Storage & Security

We take data protection seriously and implement robust technical measures to safeguard your information:

  • All passwords are hashed using industry-standard secure cryptography algorithms.
  • Data transfers between your browser and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS protocols.
  • Database access is strictly restricted to authorized system services.

⚙️ 4. User Control & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your personal information on SkillX:

  • Profile Customization: Update your bio, skills, profile picture, or avatar anytime via 👤 Profile.
  • Account Settings: Change your email, password, theme preferences, and notifications under ⚙️ Settings.
  • Account Deletion: Permanently erase your account and all associated data anytime under Settings -> Privacy -> Delete Account.

🍪 5. Cookies & Local Storage

SkillX uses browser local storage strictly for essential platform functionality:

  • Maintaining active login session state so you stay signed in safely.
  • Saving your visual appearance preference (Light Theme, Dark Theme, or System Default).
  • We do not use third-party tracking cookies or targeted advertising cookies.

🔗 6. Third-Party Services

SkillX is a student-focused learning platform. We respect your privacy:

  • We never sell, trade, or rent your personal data to advertisers or third parties.
  • Integrations with external resources (such as optional links or attachment URLs shared by users) are governed by their respective privacy terms.

📩 7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us through:

  • Help & Support Center: Visit Help & Support to submit a query.
  • Feedback: Share your thoughts via Give Feedback.
  • Email Support: Contact our team at privacy@skillx.edu.
