❓ Frequently Asked Questions
🚀 Getting Started
How does SkillX work? ▼
SkillX is a peer-to-peer student skill exchange platform. You list skills you can teach and skills you want to learn. Our Smart Matching engine pairs you with peers for mutually beneficial skill exchanges!
How do I offer or request a skill? ▼
Go to the ⚡ My Skills page from the navbar. Under "Add a Skill", select a skill from our catalogue, choose whether you want to Teach or Learn it, and click Add.
⚡ Skills & Matching
How do Smart Matches work? ▼
Our algorithm finds direct 2-way matches where a student teaches what you want to learn AND wants to learn what you teach. It also finds 1-way complementary matches to ensure you always find learning partners.
Can I teach multiple skills at once? ▼
Yes! You can add as many teaching and learning skills to your catalogue as you like. Having more skills listed increases your Smart Match recommendations.
🔄 Exchange Requests
How do I send an exchange request? ▼
Search for students on the 🔍 Search or Dashboard page and click Send Request. You can attach an optional note proposing a topic or schedule.
What happens when my request is accepted? ▼
Once a request is accepted, a direct chat channel opens in 💬 Messages, allowing you to converse, share files, and schedule 1-on-1 learning sessions!
⏱️ Time Credits & Sessions
How do Time Credits work? ▼
Every new student starts with 5 free Time Credits. When you teach a peer in a confirmed session, you earn 1 credit. When you learn from a peer, 1 credit is transferred.
How do I schedule a learning session? ▼
Open a chat with your partner in 💬 Messages and click the 📅 Schedule Session icon in the header. Choose date, time, duration, and topic.
👤 Account & Profile
How do I update my profile picture or avatar? ▼
Navigate to 👤 Profile from the top navbar. Click "Upload Photo" or choose an avatar emoji to customize your display picture across SkillX.
How do I change my theme or notification preferences? ▼
Click your name in the top navbar and select ⚙️ Settings. There you can switch between Light/Dark themes, adjust notification toggles, and set privacy rules.
⚠️ Common Problems
What if my exchange partner doesn't show up? ▼
If a partner fails to attend a scheduled session, you can cancel or reschedule the session from your chat dashboard. Unconfirmed sessions do not deduct Time Credits.
Why am I not receiving notifications? ▼
Ensure notification toggles are enabled in ⚙️ Settings -> Notifications. Check that your browser permissions allow popups/notifications for SkillX.
