👤 1. User Responsibilities

By registering and using SkillX, you agree to fulfill the following obligations:

  • Accurate Information: Provide truthful information during registration, including your name, email, department, and academic year.
  • Account Security: Keep your login credentials safe and confidential. You are responsible for all activities conducted under your account.
  • Respectful Conduct: Communicate politely and respectfully with fellow students across all chats, sessions, and platform interactions.

🤝 2. Skill Exchange Rules

SkillX is designed for peer-to-peer knowledge sharing and collaborative learning:

  • Authentic Expertise: Only list skills under "Can Teach" that you possess reasonable knowledge of and are prepared to share.
  • Punctuality & Commitment: Attend scheduled learning sessions on time and fulfill exchange agreements in good faith.
  • Educational Focus: Sessions must remain focused on educational, academic, creative, or technical skill development.

⏱️ 3. Time Credits & Sessions

Our platform uses a Time Credit system to reward active peer teachers:

  • Earning & Spending: Completing teaching sessions awards Time Credits that can be used to book learning sessions with other students.
  • No Monetary Value: Time Credits are internal platform utilities, hold zero cash value, and cannot be redeemed for real currency.
  • Cancellations: If you cannot attend a scheduled session, notify your partner as early as possible or cancel through the session menu.

To ensure a safe environment for all students, the following actions are strictly forbidden:

  • Harassment, hate speech, bullying, discrimination, or abusive behavior toward any user.
  • Academic dishonesty, including offering to take exams or write assignments for others for pay.
  • Spamming, commercial advertising, selling products, or soliciting funds off-platform.
  • Attempting to hack, scrape, reverse-engineer, or disrupt SkillX services and databases.

⚠️ 5. Account Suspension & Termination

We reserve the right to enforce community standards and protect our user base:

  • Accounts reported or detected engaging in prohibited activities may face temporary suspension or permanent termination.
  • You may close your account at any time by navigating to Settings -> Privacy -> Delete Account.
